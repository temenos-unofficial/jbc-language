How the yacc rules were rewritten

Actions, %prec and error alternatives are dropped. Left recursion such as eols : EOL | eols EOL becomes repetition. Alternatives that share a first or last symbol are factored. These one-line rules are drawn inline where they are used: comp_word, cond_body, def_target, equate_end, expand_param, opt_else, to_or_lit. The diagrams describe the token stream after jpp and the lexer, so ambiguity that the yacc precedence declarations resolve is not shown; see the table below.
